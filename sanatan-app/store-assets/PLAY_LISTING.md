## Full description (4000 chars max)
**Bhakti Daily — रोज़ की भक्ति, बिना याद रखे।**

Bhakti Daily ek saral aur sundar devotional app hai jo aapki roz ki
bhakti ko aasaan banata hai. Hanuman Chalisa, aartiyan, mantra — sab ek
jagah, **pura offline** (bina internet ke bhi chalta hai).

**✨ Khaas baatein:**
• 🚩 **Hanuman Chalisa** — pura shuddh paath, badhe aur saaf akshar mein
• 📿 **Chalisa, Aarti, Mantra** ka sangrah — Ganesh, Shiv, Durga, Lakshmi, Vishnu aur bhakti mantra
• 🔔 **Daily Bhakti Reminder** — roz apne samay par bhakti ka smaran, taaki kabhi na bhoolein
• 🔥 **Bhakti Streak** — lagataar bhakti ka hisaab aur milestones (3, 7, 21, 40, 108 din)
• 📖 **Reading mode** — akshar chota-bada, Light/Dark theme, Focus mode, "jahan chhoda tha" wahin se
• 🔎 **Search** — kisi bhi chalisa/aarti/mantra ko turant dhoondhein (offline)
• ★ **Favorites & Recently opened** — apni pasand ek tap door
• 🖼️ **Share cards** — sundar bhakti card bana kar WhatsApp/Instagram par share karein
• 📅 **Vrat & Tyohar** — aane wale tyohar ka reminder
• 🌙 **Light + Dark theme**, tablet support, tez aur halka

**Vaar ke hisaab se bhakti:** Mangalwar–Hanuman, Somwar–Shiv, Budhwar–Ganesh,
Shukrawar–Lakshmi… har din ke liye suझaav.

Bhakti Daily bilkul saral hai — koi login nahi, koi jhanjhat nahi. Bas
kholiye aur aaj ki bhakti shuru kariye. 🙏

*Note: Kuch chalisa/aarti abhi jode ja rahe hain — hum keval shuddh evं
pramanik paath hi dete hain.*
