# Privacy Policy — Bhakti Daily

_Last updated: 2026-09-08_

Bhakti Daily ("the app") is a devotional reading app. We respect your
privacy. This policy explains what data is involved.

## 1. Data the app stores on your device
The app saves the following **only on your device** (local storage), never
on our servers (we do not run any server):
- Your settings (theme, font size)
- Favorites, recently opened items, last-read position
- Bhakti streak count
- Reminder times you set

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

## 2. Notifications
The app schedules **local** daily reminders on your device. Notification
content is generated on the device. We do not send push notifications from
any server. On Android 13+ the app asks for notification permission; you can
deny or revoke it anytime in system settings.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app shows ads via **Google AdMob**. Google may collect and use data
(such as your device's advertising identifier and app usage) to serve and
measure ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings
(Settings → Privacy → Ads). A future Premium option can remove ads.

## 4. Analytics
The app records anonymous, non-personal usage events (e.g. "screen opened")
to improve the product. These contain **no personal information** and can be
turned off in Settings → Privacy.

## 5. Data we do NOT collect
We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise
location, or account information. The app has no login.

## 6. Children
The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal
data from children.

## 7. Changes
We may update this policy; changes will appear here.
