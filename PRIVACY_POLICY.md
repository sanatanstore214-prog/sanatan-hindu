Bhakti Daily ("the app", "we") is a Hindu devotional app offering chalisa, aarti, mantra,
panchang, jaap counter, rashifal, festival information and devotional status/DP images.
This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and your choices.

## 1. Information stored only on your device
The following stays **on your phone** (app local storage). We do not operate a server that
receives it:
- Display name you enter (used on blessing cards and status/DP images)
- Rashi (zodiac sign) you choose, city chosen for panchang timings
- Favorites, recently opened items, reading position, settings (theme, font size)
- Jaap counts, streak, "punya" points and level (in-app virtual points)
- Reminder times you set
- **Photo for status/DP (optional):** if you pick a photo, it is resized and kept on the
  device only to draw your image. It is never uploaded by the app. It leaves your device
  only if you choose to share or save the image you created.
- Usage events (e.g. "screen opened") used for in-app features — stored on the device
  only; they are not sent to any analytics server.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of the above.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app shows ads provided by **Google AdMob** (banner, interstitial, app-open and
rewarded ads). To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as
your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction
data, as described in Google's policies:
- How Google uses information from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Rewarded ads** are optional and shown only when you tap a button that says what you will
get. Rewards are in-app virtual items only (punya points, 24-hour access to premium image
designs, restoring a streak) and have no monetary value.

**Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland):** users in these regions are shown Google's consent form
(Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads. You can change your choice any time
in the app: Settings → "विज्ञापन गोपनीयता विकल्प" (ad privacy options).

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings
(Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads).

## 3. Family / Group Jaap (optional)
Only if you create or join a group, the app sends to a Google **Firebase Cloud Firestore**
database operated for this app:
- the group name, your chosen display name, your jaap count and update times, and a random
  member ID generated by the app (not linked to your phone number, email or advertising ID).

This information is visible to members of that group (shared total and leaderboard).
"समूह छोड़ें" (leave group) stops further updates. To delete your group data, email us
(section 9) with the group code and your display name; we delete it within 30 days.
Google processes this data under its Firebase terms: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## 4. Permissions the app uses
- **Notifications:** local daily reminders scheduled on your device (no server push).
  You can deny or turn them off in system settings.
- **Internet:** to load ads, open live darshan links, and (only if used) Group Jaap.
- **Set wallpaper / save images:** only when you tap to set or save a devotional image.
- **Photo picker:** only the photo you select is read, only for your status/DP image.
- **Foreground media playback:** to keep devotional audio playing in the background.
- Text-to-speech uses your phone's built-in speech engine.

## 5. Sharing and links
When you share a card or status, the app hands the image/text to the app you choose
(for example WhatsApp); that app's policy then applies. "Live darshan" opens official
temple streams in YouTube or your browser, which have their own policies.

## 6. What we do not do
We do not sell personal information. The app has no login and does not ask for your
email, phone number, contacts or precise location.

## 7. Security and retention
Network requests use encrypted HTTPS. On-device data is kept until you delete it or
uninstall the app. Group Jaap data is kept while the group exists or until you ask us to
delete it.

## 8. Children
Bhakti Daily is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. If you
believe a child has provided information through Group Jaap, contact us and we will delete it.
